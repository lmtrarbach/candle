## Description

Candle is a quiet place to write your novel.

Plan your story the way novelists do, then write it chapter by chapter, scene
by scene, all in one calm, distraction-free app. Candle is free and open source,
inspired by bibisco. It has no accounts, no ads and no cloud: your novels stay
on your computer.

PLAN YOUR STORY
• Architecture: premise, fabula, setting, themes and notes
• Narrative strands to follow every plot line and subplot
• Characters: main characters get an in-depth interview of about 45 questions,
  from personal data and psychology to their conflict and how they change
• Locations and objects that matter to the plot

WRITE
• Chapters and scenes, each scene with its own rich-text editor
• For every scene: status, point of view, location, story time, characters,
  strands and objects
• Full view gives the whole window to your text; Focus mode goes full screen
  with nothing but the words
• Save revisions of a scene and restore them at any time
• Light and dark themes, live word count, daily word goals

UNDERSTAND YOUR BOOK
• Timeline of scenes in story order or reading order
• Analysis: chapter lengths, and which characters, locations and strands
  appear in each chapter

EXPORT
• EPUB e-book, PDF, Word (DOCX), OpenDocument (ODT), Markdown, HTML and plain text

YOUR WORK IS SAFE
• Saved automatically as you type
• Each project is a single file you own, with automatic backups

Candle is free software under the GNU GPL v3. Source code:
https://github.com/lmtrarbach/candle
